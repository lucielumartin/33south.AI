About

About 33 South

33 South is founded and led by Lucie Martin, built on 20+ years of institutional experience, which means every engagement gets direct, senior attention from the first conversation to final delivery. As the practice grows, that same standard will extend to a small, trusted network of collaborators.

LUCIE MARTIN — FOUNDER

Lucie Martin is a strategic, delivery-oriented business leader with 20+ years of global experience spanning investment management, strategy, and business development, with deep roots in the sporting goods industry.

She spent 13 years at adidas, most recently as Senior Director, Business Development for the Emerging Markets region, leading strategic planning and cross-functional project delivery — including market entries, exits, and brand divestitures. Earlier in her adidas career, she held senior roles in Global Digital Strategy and Big Data & Sales Strategy, driving change management, OKR integration, and analytics-led decision-making at global scale.

Since 2025, Lucie has worked as an independent consultant, advising organisations including an AI SaaS voice-tech scale-up and an emotional intelligence training company on commercial development, strategy, and go-to-market execution. She holds board and advisory roles with The CoolPlay Trust and La French Tech South Africa.

33 South brings a proven ability to build high-trust partnerships and translate complex programmes into measurable impact — combining strategic rigour with hands-on delivery experience. It's built as a practice first, with the intention of growing into a small collective of specialists who share the same standard of delivery.
